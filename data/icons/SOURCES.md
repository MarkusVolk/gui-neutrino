# Sources of the icons

The icons are SVG files that Neutrino rasterises with nanosvg. White (`#e8eaee`),
grey (`#8f96a1`) and the halo colour (`#010203`) are replaced by colours of the
theme when an icon is loaded.

## Tabler Icons

The icons in the table below are [Tabler Icons](https://tabler.io/icons) 3.49.0
(outline set), Copyright (c) 2020-2026 Paweł Kuna, under the MIT License; its text
is in `LICENSE-tabler.txt`. They were changed from `currentColor` to fixed
colours, scaled to the size Neutrino draws them at and given a halo.

## Own work

All other icons in `buttons`, `filetypes`, `headers`, `hints`, `hourglass`,
`locale`, `movieplayer`, `slider`, `status`, `various` and `weather` are our own
work and dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), except `various/tux.svg`,
which came with Neutrino. Their labels are set in Liberation Sans Bold (SIL Open
Font License 1.1) and converted to outlines. The PNG files in `movieplayer` and
`weather` are rendered from these drawings.

`headers/imdb.svg`, `headers/tmdb.svg`, `various/imdb.svg` and `various/tmdb.svg`
show the names IMDb and The Movie Database, which are trademarks of their owners.
The channel logos in `logo` have their own `README.md` and `SOURCES.md`.
